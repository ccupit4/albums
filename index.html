How Similarity Is Measured

Album covers were analyzed across two visual dimensions:

  1. Color (HSV Palette): Compares the distribution of hue, color saturation, and brightness across the image.
  2. Design (Spatial Composition): Compares the spatial layout, balance of light and dark regions, and structural composition.

Scores range from -1.0 (complete opposites) to +1.0 (virtually identical).
