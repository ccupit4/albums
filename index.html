Interactive visual comparison tool for Taylor Swift and Red Hot Chili Peppers

How Similarity Is Measured

Album covers are compared across two complementary visual dimensions:

  1. Color (HSV Palette): Compares distribution of hue, color saturation, and brightness.
  2. Design (Spatial Composition): Compares spatial layout, lighting balance, and structural shapes.

Scores range from -1.0 (complete opposites) to +1.0 (virtually identical).
