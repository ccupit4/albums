1,000 Pop Album Covers: Visual Comparison

Explore how 1,000 pop album covers compare in color palette, brightness, and vibrancy

Understanding and comparing 1,000 album covers at once can be overwhelming. To make comparisons intuitive, every album cover was analyzed along two key visual axes:

  • Brightness (0–100%): From shadow-heavy, pitch-black photography to stark white minimalist backgrounds.
  • Color Saturation / Vibrancy (0–100%): From black-and-white monochrome to neon, hyper-saturated palettes.
